Parking & Access

This site requires a Sunnyvale keycard specific to each operator. If you do not have a keycard and are scheduled for a Sunnyvale meeting, please reach out to @Sydney Bucek as soon as possible.

The city hall has plenty of parking on the south side of the building, off Olive Ave and S. Mathilda Ave. See the map below.

Meeting Instructions

Before Meeting

1Arrive and check in

  1. Use your keycard to enter the control room. A city employee may already be on site. If not, turn on the lights using the switch to the right of the door.
  2. Wake up the Operator PC using the keyboard and mouse on the shelf underneath the control desk.
  3. Using Microsoft Teams on the Operator PC, find the appropriate group chat (either “Planning Commission” or “Council Meeting”) and let the staff know that you have arrived.
    “Hey, this is <name> from KMVT. I’ve arrived and I am setting up.”
  4. Confirm that the latest agenda is printed out on the desk. You will want to reference this later. If it is not present on arrival, a city employee will likely bring it soon.

Keep your keycard on your person at all times.

The control room door locks automatically. Wear your keycard around your neck, or keep it in a secure pocket. If you leave it on the control desk, you will be locked out eventually.

NEW PHOTOS AND INSTRUCTIONS NEEDED FOR THIS SEGMENT

3Schedule record and stream events

  1. Using the Operator PC, open Google Chrome and navigate to https://playback.sv.kmvt.net or use the Hypercaster shortcut.
  2. Log in using username psguser and password psguser .
  3. Navigate to the classic programming tab via Programming > Classic.
  4. Click on Add Stream Event.
  5. Fill out the stream event details as follows, and hit Save. All other fields can be left as is.
    ChannelKSUN Sunnyvale 16
    FeedCouncil Chambers
    Duration10:00:00
    DateTodays date
    TimeTwo minutes before meeting starts (24 hour time, HH:MM:SS format)
    Cancel Conflicting EventsYes / Checked
  6. Return to Programming > Classic and then Add Record Event.
  7. Fill out the recording details as follows, and hit Save. All other fields can be left as is.
    ChannelCapture 1
    FeedCouncil Chambers
    FilenameIf planning commission: svpcYYMMDD
    If city council meeting: svccYYMMDD

    For example, for a city council meeting on 07/14/2026, use svcc260714 as the filename.
    DateTodays date
    TimeTwo minutes before meeting starts (24 hour time, HH:MM:SS format)
    Duration10:00:00
  8. Return to the Dashboard and verify that the stream and record events appear with the correct date and time.
